# Social Creative Strategy

Use this skill for brand-aligned social and campaign creative planning.

This skill provides methodology and creative guidance only. It does not authorize publishing, scheduling, social account access, scraping, social listening, shell execution, or paid media generation.

## Authoritative Inputs

Prefer:
- verified Brand Identity
- verified Design System
- project requirements
- ResearchPackage evidence
- CopyPackage or approved messaging
- customer-wide reusable assets
- project-specific assets
- campaign objective

Do not invent brand voice, customer claims, performance data, platform facts, or asset IDs.

## Content Pillars

Define a repeatable set of content pillars aligned with:
- business goals
- audience needs
- brand positioning
- evidence-backed topics
- conversion objectives
- creative variety

Useful pillar families may include:
- educational
- product/value
- customer problem
- case study
- thought leadership
- behind the scenes
- community
- campaign promotion

## Hooks

Use hooks to clarify value quickly, not to fabricate urgency or unsupported claims.

Useful patterns:
- curiosity
- problem/pain
- clear benefit
- surprising evidence-backed insight
- story entry
- contrarian position when supported
- data/evidence callout

Never invent statistics, results, quotes, testimonials, or customer outcomes.

## Carousels

Use a clear narrative arc. A typical structure:
1. hook
2. problem or context
3. core insight or framework
4. supporting points
5. proof/example when available
6. CTA

Each slide should perform one primary communication job.

Do not copy competitor creatives. Extract reusable narrative patterns and adapt them to the customer's brand.

## Repurposing

Adapt strong source content rather than duplicating it unchanged.

Possible source material:
- blog post
- webinar
- podcast
- product demo
- case study
- newsletter
- research report
- long-form video

Possible derived assets:
- social post
- carousel
- short-form video
- thumbnail
- quote graphic
- story banner
- display banner
- email banner

Preserve factual meaning and source provenance. Adapt messaging, format, aspect ratio, duration, and visual hierarchy for each placement.

## Platform Adaptation

Do not assume one creative should be used unchanged everywhere.

Adapt where relevant:
- aspect ratio
- duration
- text density
- hook timing
- CTA
- caption structure
- visual safe areas
- motion pace
- subtitle requirements
- muted-playback compatibility

Do not hard-code platform limits or algorithm claims as permanent facts unless supplied by verified current data.

## Short-Form Video

For short-form video briefs define:
- objective
- audience
- hook
- core message
- CTA
- duration
- aspect ratio
- scenes
- pacing
- on-screen text
- captions
- visual direction
- motion direction
- sound direction
- muted-playback compatibility
- reference asset IDs
- preservation requirements

The brief should communicate value quickly and still make sense without sound where relevant.

## Reference Guidance

Use existing customer assets and previous creatives as style/brand context when authorized.

Reference roles may include:
- SUBJECT
- PRODUCT
- PERSON
- LOCATION
- STYLE
- COMPOSITION
- COLOR_PALETTE
- BRAND
- LOGO
- BACKGROUND
- LIGHTING
- POSE
- WARDROBE

Preserve explicit preservation requirements. Do not expose raw storage paths or credentials.

## Variants

Plan variants by placement and purpose rather than making arbitrary duplicates.

Variant dimensions may include:
- platform
- placement
- aspect ratio
- hook
- CTA
- copy density
- crop
- visual emphasis
- duration
- audience segment when supported

Keep each variant traceable to its source creative/specification.

## Measurement

Define measurement recommendations when useful, such as:
- reach
- engagement
- saves
- shares
- clicks
- leads
- conversions
- completion rate for video

Do not invent analytics or performance results.
