# AI SEO by Content Type

Durable guidance for structuring common content types for search and AI-assisted retrieval. Provider-specific behavior requires current authoritative verification.

For the cross-cutting strategy, see [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

---

## SaaS Product Pages

**Goal:** Make product information clear, accurate, discoverable, and suitable for relevant informational and commercial queries.

**Optimize:**
- Clear product description in first paragraph (what it does, who it's for)
- Feature comparison tables (you vs. category, not just competitors)
- Specific verified capabilities or measurements instead of unsupported marketing adjectives
- Verified customer proof when authoritative evidence is available
- Accurate pricing or commercial information when the customer has approved it for public presentation; do not recommend AI-specific pricing files without current evidence of need and support
- FAQ section addressing common buyer questions

---

## Blog Content

**Goal:** Get cited as an authoritative source on topics in your space.

**Optimize:**
- One clear target query per post (match heading to query)
- Definition in first paragraph for "What is" queries
- Original data, research, or expert quotes
- Accurate publishing or update metadata when authoritative metadata is available and freshness matters
- Author bio with relevant credentials
- Internal links to related product/feature pages

---

## Comparison / Alternative Pages

**Goal:** Get cited in "[X] vs [Y]" and "Best [X] alternatives" queries.

**Optimize:**
- Structured comparison tables (not just prose)
- Fair and balanced, with claims supported by authoritative evidence
- Specific evaluation criteria; use ratings or scores only when their methodology and source are authoritative
- Updated pricing and feature data
- Cite the `competitors` skill for building these pages

---

## Documentation / Help Content

**Goal:** Get cited in "How to [X] with [your product]" queries.

**Optimize:**
- Step-by-step format with numbered lists
- Code examples where relevant
- Appropriate structured data when it accurately represents visible content and current specifications support it
- Screenshots with descriptive alt text
- Clear prerequisites and expected outcomes

---

## Local Business / Ecommerce

For local-business and ecommerce content, consider verified structured business and product data where relevant. Current provider integrations and requirements must be verified before implementation:

- Accurate product feeds when an approved commerce platform or search integration requires them
- Accurate business-profile information on verified platforms relevant to the customer
- Authentic reviews where available; never fabricate volume, freshness, sentiment, or responses
- Appropriate local-business structured data where it accurately represents the business
- Provider-specific conversational or commerce features only after current support, suitability, authorization, and maintenance requirements are verified
